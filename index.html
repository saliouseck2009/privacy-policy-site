Privacy Policy — Wolof Quran

Last updated: April 11, 2026

Wolof Quran does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Your privacy is fully respected.

Data stored on your device

All your data stays on your device and is never sent to any server:

  • Your preferences (language, theme, font size, selected reciter)
  • Downloaded audio files
  • Bookmarks and reading progress

Internet access

The app uses an internet connection solely to:

  • Download Quran audio recitations
  • Check for app updates

No personal information is sent during these operations.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions strictly necessary for its features:

  • Internet: to download audio content
  • Audio playback: to play Quran recitations, including in the background

Suitable for all ages

Since the app does not collect any personal data, it is safe for users of all ages, including children.
