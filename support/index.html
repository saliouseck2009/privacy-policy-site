Frequently asked questions

How do I download audio?

Go to Settings > Audio & Tafsir Authors, select a reciter, then download the surahs you want. An internet connection is required for the first download.

Audio is not playing?

Make sure the surah is downloaded, check your volume, and try restarting the app. If the issue persists, delete the audio and re-download it.

How do I change the app language?

Go to Settings > Language and choose your preferred language.

How do I report a bug?

Send an email to the address above with a description of the problem, your device model, and the app version (found in Settings > About).
